# Phone Screenshots Placeholder

Screenshots must be captured from a running Android device or emulator before Google Play Store submission.

## Fastlane Usage

Fastlane Supply uploads images from this folder in alphabetical order. Name your screenshots with numeric prefixes to ensure the intended display order in the Play Store listing:

- `1_home.png` — Home screen with sobriety day counter and milestone progress
- `2_sos_wave.png` — Craving SOS: Ride the Wave urge-surfing timer and soundscapes
- `3_sos_breathe.png` — Craving SOS: 4-4-4-4 Box Breathing
- `4_log_heatmap.png` — Daily check-in log and craving heatmap
- `5_cbt_thought_check.png` — CBT Thought Check and cognitive distortions guide
- `6_stats.png` — Streak stats and health/recovery timeline

## Google Play Requirements

- **File format:** PNG (recommended: 24-bit PNG without alpha) or JPEG
- **Quantity:** At least 2 screenshots, up to 8 screenshots
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 or 9:16 (or any aspect ratio between 1:2 and 2:1)
- **Dimensions:** Minimum 320 px, maximum 3840 px on any side
  - Common resolutions: 1080x1920, 1080x2340, 1080x2400, or 1440x3120
- **Maximum file size:** 8 MB per screenshot
