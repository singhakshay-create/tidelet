Built for the hard moments and the steady days.

Every feature in Tidelet is grounded in cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT), Alan Marlatt's relapse-prevention framework, and urge surfing. All functionality runs entirely on your device.

Immediate Relief

In the Moment: Craving SOS

Fast, focused tools designed to ground your nervous system and help you ride out an urge until it subsides.

  • Ride the Wave: A 15-minute urge-surfing timer with an animated wave and your choice of ambient soundscapes (Ocean, River, Rain, or Silent).
  • Box Breathing: A 4-4-4-4 paced breathing exercise with cycle counting to settle autonomic arousal.
  • My Reasons: A personal, editable list of why you started, readily accessible when willpower feels thin.
  • Do Something Else: A curated catalog of healthy alternative actions with a "pick one for me" shuffle button.
  • Thought Check: A CBT-style thought-record tool to examine and dismantle craving-related thoughts in the moment.
  • Craving Journal: Free-form reflection space to write during or right after an urge.
  • Compassionate Reset: Log a slip ("I drank") without punishing language or shame. Your streak history and journal entries are never erased.
Cognitive Practice

Evidence-Based CBT Tools

Structured exercises to identify automatic negative thoughts, build refusal skills, and prevent future slips.

  • Cognitive Distortions Library: Learn to recognize common thought traps (such as all-or-nothing thinking or emotional reasoning) alongside targeted antidotes for each.
  • Refusal Rehearsal: Guided mental practice for firmly turning down drinks in social gatherings and high-pressure settings.
  • Relapse-Prevention Plan: Map out personal warning signs, high-risk situations, and step-by-step coping strategies before challenges arise.
  • Categorized Thought History: Browse previous thought records organized by distortion type to recognize recurring mental habits.
Long-Term Progress

Tracking Your Progress

Observe patterns over time with honest, non-judgmental tracking tools that respect your privacy.

  • Day Counter & Milestones: Track your current streak alongside clear progress toward your next milestone.
  • Daily Log & Craving Heatmap: A daily check-in form with an intensity heatmap to discover your peak urge times and triggers.
  • Weekly Reflection & Evening Reviews: Guided end-of-week reviews and evening check-ins to evaluate what worked and what felt difficult.
  • Milestone Letters: Write personal letters to your future self at milestones, locked until you reach the next stage of your journey.
  • Stats & Health Timeline: View streak metrics and an evidence-backed timeline tracking physical and neurological recovery.
  • Home-Screen Widget: Keep your streak visible at a glance without having to open the app.
Privacy & Control

Local & in Your Control

No accounts, no sync, no subscriptions. Everything you enter remains stored on your own physical phone.

  • Zero Network Permissions: Tidelet requests no internet access in its Android manifest. It is technically incapable of sending data anywhere.
  • Customizable Baseline: Set your typical drinks per day and price to calculate money and time reclaimed, formatted in your local currency.
  • Full Data Export & Verification: Export your data to human-readable files on your device with a one-tap backup integrity check.
  • Getting Started Guide: An in-app orientation walking you through each tool and its clinical foundation.
  • Community & Crisis Resources: Direct shortcuts to helplines and peer groups (AA, SMART Recovery, Moderation Management, r/stopdrinking) via your phone's dialer or browser.

A quiet, distraction-free interface.

Designed to feel calm and unobtrusive when your nervous system is overwhelmed. No bright gamification badges, no social feeds, and no noisy push notifications.

Privacy is an architecture, not a policy.

Most "private" wellness apps mean "we encrypt what we collect." Tidelet does not collect anything in the first place.

•

No backend server: Tidelet doesn't talk to the internet at all.

•

No user accounts: Nobody signs up, nobody logs in, and no personal email or identity is requested.

•

No analytics or telemetry: Nothing about how you use the app is measured, recorded, or sent anywhere.

•

No ad SDKs: Tidelet doesn't show ads and doesn't embed any ad or tracking libraries.

•

Zero network permissions: The Android app requests no network-related permissions — it is technically incapable of making a network call. You can verify this in app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml in the source code; there is no INTERNET permission declared.

Not Medical Advice & Crisis Resources

Tidelet is a self-help tool for managing cravings and tracking your own progress. It is not a medical device, it does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it is not a substitute for professional care. If you are struggling, please reach out to a real person:

  • US: call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or call the SAMHSA National Helpline at 1-800-662-4357 (free, confidential, 24/7).
  • Outside the US: please look up your local crisis line or emergency number — Tidelet's Resources screen lists a few country-specific helplines, but if yours isn't listed, a quick search for "[your country] crisis line" or "[your country] suicide prevention helpline" will get you there.
  • If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.
